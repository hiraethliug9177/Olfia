# Olfia — instructions for coding agents

Read `docs/PRD.md`, `docs/DESIGN-SPEC.md`, and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` before changing anything. This file is the short version.

- Stack: Next.js 15 App Router, React 19, TypeScript strict, Tailwind v4 (tokens in `app/globals.css` `@theme`), zustand + localStorage, html-to-image, GA4 via gtag.js (`components/Analytics.tsx`, events in `lib/analytics.ts`). npm. Node 22.
- Commands: `npm run dev`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run build`. Type errors fail the build; run typecheck before finishing.
- Terms, exactly: **Scent Scene**, **Rooms**, **Scent Atlas**, **Scent Card** (renamed from Scene Card on 2026-11-03). Copy v1 (2026-11-24) adds Tiffany's names: **Capture a scent**, **Scent Journal**, **Favorites**, **My Scentprint**, **Fragrance Shelf**, **Fragrance Matches**, **The Olfia Edit**, **Scentpedia**, **For myself / For someone else**, **Join the waitlist**; Rooms' views (Notion 11/23, plans table): **Room View**, **Scent View**, **Photo View**. Never use: Scent Profile, Room Profile, Scent Direction (as a feature name), Experience Record, Tags & profile, My shelf.
- Copy source of truth: `docs/copy/olfia-site-copy-v1.md`, with `olfia-brand-voice-guide.md` and `olfia-copy-change-log.md` (US spelling, sentence-case buttons, no neighbour-layering claims, keep recorded frequency, chosen preference and suggested family apart). Long texts live in `lib/copy/*.ts`, generated verbatim from the doc; never render [NEW COPY], [VERIFY] or R numbers.
- Design: ivory canvas, charcoal text; `periwinkle` (#A0A6C8) is decorative only — never text or meaningful icons; interactive colour is `periwinkle-deep` (#4C527A). Fonts (three only, 2026-11-10): Newsreader (titles, work names), IBM Plex Mono (navigation, body, questions, buttons, dates, charts), Caveat Bold (notes and quick handwriting). No all-caps labels, no single italic word in headings. Icons: Lucide only, see `AGENTS.md`. Minimum text 13px. Signature double frame only on the Rooms stage and Scent Card.
- **Colour rule (Tiffany 11/10 — do not forget):** every interface colour (buttons, panels, sections, menus, tabs, footer) comes only from the brand guide of 11/3: Ivory #F8F6F0, White #FFFFFF, Mist #ECEDF5, Charcoal #292A31, Slate #5C5E6B, Periwinkle deep #4C527A (buttons, links, selected), Periwinkle #A0A6C8 and Apricot #E7B49F (washes; apricot is "the pink"). The seven family colours appear only where they stand for a scent: family dots, chips and highlights, Scent Library sections, scene pages, stamps, the Scent Card picture, quiz characters' rooms. Never use a family colour for a button or a section background. The footer's background is Tiffany's own halftone image (11/17); its panel and links keep the brand colours.
- Rooms: one fixed front view per scene, free drag placement (since v3), morning/afternoon/night light; no multiple angles, no true 3D. Three views since 11/24: Room View (editor, notes tags: product line + notes line + leader to a ring), Scent View (canvas motion per product type with dashed rings, `components/rooms/ScentFlow.tsx`; five sensory sliders, Subtle–Intense = Scent strength), Photo View (room photo ↔ collage). Every room shows a bar above it (Choose a feeling → Choose a space → Name your room; the six spaces are the Home presets, and there is no separate Room setting or fragrance choice since her 11/24 evening notes); Scent families fold into one row; Rooms' guide lines float as notes with × (`olfia.tips.v1`); Fragrance Matches are wide cards with Add to room · Save to Atlas · Explore fragrance and come before Your rooms. Since the 11/24 handoff Rooms (atmosphere workshop: tray · room · panel · bar, `.ws*`) and Scent Card (memory-making desk: card · Start with + Style/Decorate/Details · bar, `.desk*`) each have their own grid; history steps hold the whole room (`Snap` in RoomEditor), and anything that replaces the room asks first when there are unsaved changes. Saved Scent Card images come from an off-screen 360 px copy (1080 × 1920 / 1440). Fragrance objects and their Scent View types in `lib/catalog.ts`; notes, colours and matching in `lib/roomScent.ts`. Catalog in `content/scenes.json`, types in `lib/types.ts`.
- The Olfia Edit masthead (`components/edit/Masthead.tsx`, 11/24 evening): her line, the nameplate and a date stamp wheel; a daily random family, a daily public-domain motto (`lib/mottos.ts`, published before 1929 and checked against a source; keep it that way) and the ISO week as the edition, all read in the browser.
- Data: entries in localStorage key `olfia.atlas.v2` (v3 build), shape in `lib/types.ts`; every other browser key also starts with `olfia.` (`lib/localData.ts` exports and clears them; keep the Privacy page's list in step). No accounts, no server storage in this release. Typed text (names, notes, tags) never goes in a URL.
- Do not add: accounts, databases, payments, social features, health or safety claims, new product terms. Photo upload exists only in Scent Atlas → Fragrance Shelf (Tiffany 11/10) and Rooms → Photo View (her answer 8, 11/23; `olfia.roomphoto.v1`); both shrink photos and keep them in this browser.
- Content strings are Tiffany's. If copy is missing, insert a clear `TODO(Tiffany)` placeholder rather than inventing brand copy.
- Keep `docs/DECISIONS.md` updated with one line per decision; log AI sessions in `docs/AI-USE-LOG.md`.
